## 1. What CoopLaunch Malta is

**CoopLaunch Malta** is a live website used to run a **12-week cooperative founding pilot in Malta**.

Website: **https://coop.mihai.com.mt**

Purpose:
- Help Nesli (Coordinator) and the Industry Founder work through a structured 12-week plan
- Track tasks, people to contact, interviews, money assumptions, decisions, documents, and co-op registration readiness
- Keep progress evidence-based: finish checklists before moving weeks forward (or record a clear reason to skip)

It is **not** the Mihai volume calculator (`calc.mihai.com.mt`). It is a separate app on the same server.

Sector focus for this live pilot: **construction** (builders, electrical, plumbing, materials suppliers, etc.).  
Food / hospitality / logistics sample contacts were removed. Contacts should stay empty until real people are added, or only clearly fake construction examples if needed for demos.

## 4. Big picture workflow (how the pilot works)

Think of CoopLaunch as a guided workbook:

1. **Home** — see today and what is waiting  
2. **Contacts** — list companies/people to talk to  
3. **Interviews** — write what they said and tag problems  
4. **12-week plan** — finish weekly tasks and checklists  
5. **Founder tasks** — Nesli asks the founder to do concrete actions  
6. **Money & value** — prices, assumptions, member savings ideas  
7. **Decisions & docs** — advice, meetings, decisions, risks, uploads  
8. **Register co-op** — checklist before legal registration  

Core rule of the 12-week plan:
- Each task has a **checklist**
- Mark checklist items **done**
- Then **Mark task finished**
- At week end, finish the **week checklist**, then **Go to next week**  
- If something must be skipped, use **Skip remaining items** and type a reason

## 7. 12-week timeline (what each week is for)

| Week | Title | Purpose in plain words |
|------|-------|------------------------|
| 1 | Kick-off & framing | Align on purpose and working rhythm; start charter |
| 2 | Stakeholder map | Decide who to contact and interview |
| 3 | Problem discovery | Do interviews and note recurring problems |
| 4 | Problem Matrix | Rank top problems from interview tags |
| 5 | Founder fit | Careful notes on founder strengths and questions to explore |
| 6 | Solution options | Shortlist solutions with evidence |
| 7 | Supplier scan | Find comparable supplier offers |
| 8 | Economics | Record money assumptions and scenarios |
| 9 | Member value | Draft what a member might gain |
| 10 | Governance pack | Keep advice, risks, decisions current |
| 11 | Registration pack | Open registration checklist and list blockers |
| 12 | Go / no-go | Final review: ready to register or not |

How progress works:
- A week is **This week** while in progress
- Tasks move from Not started → In progress → Finished
- Completing the week checklist (or skipping with reason) unlocks the next week

Current live state may already have Week 1 completed and Week 2 in progress from pilot testing. Nesli should treat the on-screen week status as truth.

## 8. What “CRM / Contacts” means (for Nesli)

**CRM** = Customer/Relationship list. In this app the menu name is **Contacts**.

Use it to store:
- Construction companies (builders, electricians, plumbers, materials suppliers, etc.)
- People at those companies (name, role, email)
- Status of the relationship (contacted, interested, do not contact, etc.)

Typical Nesli flow:
1. Add company in Contacts  
2. Add person linked to that company  
3. Interview them and save notes in Interviews  
4. Use interview tags to see common problems  
5. Ask founder to help with introductions or decisions via **Send ask**

## 9. Registration checklist (Register co-op)

The registration page tracks readiness percentage and required blockers.

Typical seeded items include things like:
- Draft statutes
- Founder identification documents
- Bank introduction letter (often optional)

Rules Nesli should remember:
- **Required** items need both “done” and a supporting uploaded file
- Until required items are complete with files, readiness stays blocked under 100%
- Upload files first in **Decisions & docs**, then attach them on **Register co-op**
